<h1>Qryptana is an early Labs-stage private communication project.</h1>
<p>Qryptana is a downstream fork derived from SimpleX Chat. It preserves the upstream open-source messaging implementation while developing an independent product identity.</p>
<p>SimpleX Chat is the upstream project and protocol implementation. Its messaging features include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>end-to-end encrypted messages with editing, replies and deletion;</li>
  <li>end-to-end encrypted images and files;</li>
  <li>single-use and long-term user addresses;</li>
  <li>private chat groups;</li>
  <li>end-to-end encrypted audio and video calls;</li>
  <li>portable chat profiles.</li>
</ul>
<p>Qryptana does not currently provide official public downloads or claim independent security assessments, server operation, support channels or release infrastructure. Do not treat upstream SimpleX documentation, services or releases as Qryptana commitments.</p>
